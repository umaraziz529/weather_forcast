Weather where you are

When you press the button, your browser will ask for location permission. If you allow it, your coordinates are sent to this site's server and used to load local weather.

Accuracy depends on your device, browser and available signals (GPS, Wi-Fi, mobile network). It can be off by metres or by kilometres.

    Privacy notice

    After you allow location using the Detect button, your coordinates are sent to this site's server at /api/location and kept in memory until the server stops; they are not written to disk or exposed through a public read endpoint. Coordinates are also sent to Open-Meteo (weather data) and BigDataCloud (to find a place name). Typed city names go to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. There is no background location. If you deny permission, the app will not send your device location; use the manual search instead.
